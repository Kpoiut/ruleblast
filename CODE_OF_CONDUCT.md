# Code of Conduct

Hard evidence. Soft people.

RuleBlast is a place to bring surprising repository-instruction results, not a place to win arguments. We protect the person who filed the weird blast as carefully as we protect the bytes. Contributors build evidence about repository instruction projection, and the same evidence-first standard applies to how people are treated here.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, patient, and constructive.
- Challenge claims with reproducible evidence rather than attacking the person making them.
- Preserve uncertainty and say when evidence is incomplete.
- Respect privacy: remove credentials, private source contents, identifying local paths, and personal information from reports.
- Accept a maintainer's decision to narrow or close work that crosses the public product boundary.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private material, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers when a private GitHub channel is visibly available. Otherwise, open a minimal public request for private contact without naming people or disclosing sensitive details. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation to protect contributors and the project.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, safety, proportionality, and the privacy of everyone involved. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, review comments, and project-related communication in which someone represents RuleBlast.
